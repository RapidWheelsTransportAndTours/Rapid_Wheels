About us

A Montego Bay team that knows the roads.

Rapid Wheels Transport And Tours is based in Montego Bay on Jamaica's north-west coast. We drive visitors and locals to the airport, to their hotels and to the island's best-loved places.

Why people book with us

You deal directly with the people who drive. Message us on WhatsApp, tell us the plan, and get a clear answer on price and pickup time before you commit.

Built around your plans

Services

Rides for every kind of trip.

Share your group size when you ask for a quote and we match the vehicle to your party.

Airport transfers

Sangster International Airport (MBJ) to hotels, villas and resorts in Montego Bay, Negril, Falmouth, Ocho Rios and beyond. Share your flight details so we plan around your landing time.

Hotel & resort transfers

Rides between your accommodation and the places you want to go, one way or return.

Private island tours

Full-day and half-day trips with a driver who stays with you. Pick a tour from our list or build your own route.

Cruise port transfers

Pickups from the Montego Bay and Falmouth cruise ports for a day on shore, with a return in time for boarding.

Groups & events

Weddings, church outings, school trips, reunions and company travel. Larger groups are welcome.

Day hire

Keep a driver for the day for shopping, meetings, errands or a long lunch. You set the stops.
