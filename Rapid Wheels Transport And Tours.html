About Rapid Wheels

A Jamaican transport and tour company built around one idea: you should never worry about how you are getting there.

Rapid Wheels Transport And Tours moves visitors, families, teams and groups across the island. We began with airport pickups and grew into full-day tours and private charters because our customers kept asking for more.

Every trip is planned around you. Tell us how many people are travelling, where you are staying and what you want to see, and we match the right vehicle and driver.

We care about safe driving, honest prices and being easy to reach. When plans change, you can message us and we adjust.

Questions we hear a lot

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. For airport pickups and weekend or holiday tours, a few days ahead is best. We try to help with short notice too.

What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when you book. We track arrivals and adjust the pickup time with no extra charge for normal delays.

Can the driver wait while we visit a place?

Yes. Tours include wait time at each stop. For charters, wait time is part of the hourly rate.

Do you carry child seats?

Yes, please ask when you book so we have the right size ready.

Which areas do you cover?

All parishes, with regular runs between Kingston, Ocho Rios, Montego Bay, Negril, Portland and the south coast.

How do I pay?

We confirm payment options when we send your quote.

What if I need to cancel or change my trip?

Message us as soon as plans change. The earlier you tell us, the easier it is to move your booking.
